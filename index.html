How it finds the best fit

Most GIF makers compress hard and hope. This one works down a ladder — cheapest quality loss first — and stops the moment it fits.

The optimiser ladder — with an estimator

  1. Estimate first: from your clip's width, height, length, frame rate and how detailed the captured frames are, FitGif estimates the GIF size for every ladder step and picks the step closest to 10 MB
  2. Jump straight there: it starts at that predicted step — often step 3 or 4 — instead of crawling down from maximum quality every time. You see the prediction before you tap Create, and a refined prediction after your frames are captured
  3. Then go up or down: if that step is over the limit, it steps down to lighter settings; if it fits with room to spare, it steps up to try better quality. It keeps the best version that fits
  4. Frame rate, then resolution: steps lower frame rate gradually, then shrink width in small steps, then further down to 200px, 160px and below if a longer window needs it
  5. Every step is lighter: each ladder step is at least 10% lighter than the one above it, so stepping down marches the file size down — never up
  6. Length is set by your size: about 1 minute per 10 MB. Within that window, duration is locked — quality steps down to fit, the window is never shortened further
  7. Keep the best: stop when stepping up no longer fits, or at the first fit when stepping down — that version under 10 MB is the winner

Good to know

Formats: MP4, WebM, MOV and most browser-supported video.

Max length follows your size: about 1 minute per 10 MB — 5 MB = 30s, 8 MB = 48s, 10 MB = 1:00, 15 MB = 1:30, 25 MB = 2:30, 50 MB = 5:00, 100 MB Pro = 10:00. A clip shorter than your max is used whole, exactly as uploaded.

Got a long video? Upload something like 20 minutes and a timeline scrubber appears at the top of the dashboard. Scan the whole video, pick a window of your max length, and that window — kept whole — is optimised to fit. There is no separate trim step; the scrub bar is the trim.

Hard limit: anything over your limit is discarded. Only a GIF under the limit — 10,485,760 bytes at 10 MB — is ever offered for download.

You stay in control: set the maximum width (up to 720px) and frame rate (up to 20 fps) in FitGif. It only goes below those if it has to fit the full length.

Save it two ways: download it to your Downloads folder, or tap Save to Photos — that opens FitGif's own save screen (not just a share sheet), saves a backup copy to Downloads so the GIF is never lost, and shows the GIF big so you can press-and-hold to put it straight into Photos / gallery.
