FitGif makes a GIF from your video that fits the size limit of the site you want to post it on — optimised, compressed and AI tweaked to the best quality that fits, with the whole clip kept. The length is never trimmed.

How it works

  1. Add your video — one tap, from your camera roll or files.
  2. Pick your size — one press and it starts. Your video plays while the GIF is made.
  3. Save screen. You land on the finished GIF with its preview — Save it, put it in Photos, or share it. Short clips are used whole. For longer videos, about a minute of video fits per 10 MB — slide the window over the filmstrip to pick the part you want, and that part is kept whole.
